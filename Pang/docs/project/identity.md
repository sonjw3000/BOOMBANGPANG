# Project Identity

Universe Logistics is a logistics and warehouse automation management simulation game set during the early expansion of humanity into the solar system.

Human civilization has only recently become an interplanetary society. 
Remote colonies depend heavily on unstable logistics infrastructure, long-distance cargo transport, and frontier-style supply chains.

The player operates a logistics hub responsible for receiving, storing, processing, and shipping cargo across planetary settlements from the moon.

The setting combines industrial logistics gameplay with near-future sci-fi elements such as:
- cargo spacecraft
- hazardous landings
- vacuum environments
- low-gravity accidents
- fragile infrastructure
- frontier colony logistics

Space environments should influence gameplay and incidents in believable ways.

For example:
- failed landings may scatter debris across facilities
- explosions in vacuum environments behave differently from Earth
- infrastructure damage may disrupt logistics flow
- dangerous logistics events may emerge from space transportation itself

The core gameplay focuses on:
- bottleneck management
- throughput optimization
- logistics flow stabilization
- balancing human labor and automation

The project prioritizes:
- readable simulation
- visible logistics flow
- operational clarity
- debuggable systems

over:
- excessive realism
- hidden calculations
- overengineered systems
- fully autonomous gameplay

The game is designed around the feeling of turning operational chaos into a stable and efficient interplanetary logistics system.
